🌟 Tribute to Dr. APJ Abdul Kalam

The Missile Man of India | Former President | Visionary Scientist

About Dr. Kalam

Dr. Avul Pakir Jainulabdeen Abdul Kalam (1931-2015) was an eminent Indian scientist, aerospace engineer, and the 11th President of India. Known as the “Missile Man of India”, he played a pivotal role in India’s civilian space program and military missile development. He was also a beloved teacher and inspirational leader who motivated millions of youth to dream big.

Timeline of Achievements

  • 1931: Born in Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu.
  • 1960: Joined Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO).
  • 1980: Successfully launched India’s first Satellite Launch Vehicle (SLV-III).
  • 1992: Appointed Chief Scientific Adviser to the Prime Minister.
  • 2002-2007: Served as 11th President of India.
  • 2015: Passed away while delivering a lecture in Shillong, inspiring millions.
